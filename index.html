Machine Learning · Partial Differential Equations · Optimal Recovery · Reduced Order Modeling · Data Assimilation

Stiven Briand Massala

I develop algorithms and theory for solving partial differential equations, combining machine learning, reduced order modeling, optimal recovery and variational data assimilation. I completed my PhD jointly at École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay and Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

Before my PhD, I earned a Diplôme d’Ingénieur in mechanical engineering from Arts et Métiers ParisTech and a Master of Research in applied mathematics from CentraleSupélec, Université Paris-Saclay, focused on the modelling and simulation of partial differential equations.
